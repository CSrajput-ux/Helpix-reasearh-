# Contributor Code of Conduct

As contributors and maintainers of the HELPix-R project, we pledge to respect all people who contribute through open issues, submitting pull requests, asking questions, or giving feedback.

## Scientific Integrity
Contributors are expected to adhere to high standards of scientific reproducibility, reporting transparency, and ethical conduct. Under no circumstances should results, synthetic benchmark numbers, or misleading performance claims be submitted.

## Academic & Open Source Collaboration
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all, regardless of level of experience, gender, sexual orientation, disability, personal appearance, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.
